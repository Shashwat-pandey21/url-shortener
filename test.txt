URL Shortener

Design a URL shortener service that:
1. Accepts a valid URL.
2. Generates a short URL.
3. Redirects users to the original URL.
4. Tracks total visits/clicks.

Routes:

POST /url
- Create a short URL.

GET /:id
- Redirect to original URL.

GET /url/analytics/:id
- Return total clicks/analytics.
